Why Hopper Day?

Hopper and her team worked on early compilers and FLOW-MATIC, helping programmers use words in place of machine instructions. That work influenced COBOL. Her oral history is a good place to meet the person behind the name.

We're choosing August 14 for Hopper Day because that's when Omarchy 4, Quattro, shipped in 2026. For me, that release made working with a computer in plain language feel worth celebrating. I'd like to see what it means to you.

A useful computer deserves another job

Windows 11's hardware requirements left many working PCs outside the upgrade path. PIRG estimated up to 400 million machines were affected, representing up to 1.6 billion pounds of potential e-waste. That's an estimate of the hardware at risk, not a count of computers already dumped.

The deadline had consequences before it arrived. Omdia reported a wave of PC upgrades and replacements ahead of October 2025. By early 2026, much of the near-term commercial refresh pipeline was already exhausted.

Microsoft later extended consumer security updates to October 12, 2027, months after regular support ended. That buys time for remaining PCs. It doesn't undo replacement decisions already made. Businesses and schools have a separate extended-update program.

There is still useful work for these machines. Linux can give compatible hardware another life, including as a client for an AI agent. If you're curious about Omarchy, start with its installation guide and agent setup. Compatibility, setup time, and any AI service costs depend on your machine and the tools you choose.

This compares worldwide AI investment with selected US budgets. It shows scale; it doesn't measure everything spent on these fields or prove that one budget took money from another. The research notes include the calculations, more comparisons, and their limits.

I want more of the computing power being built to reach people working on those problems. Researchers, teachers, artists, and people keeping a small organization going should have a say in what these tools are for.

Using an old laptop doesn't erase the cost of a cloud service. AI server demand is also putting pressure on memory supplies for PCs. Keeping hardware in use can avoid a replacement purchase. It's one practical thing we can do while asking harder questions about the buildout.

The opportunity

Start with something you care about. Revisit research you set aside, catalog a collection, or build a tool you've wanted for years. That's what I want to celebrate with Hopper Day: more people getting to make things with their computers.

I enjoy the mechanics of making things work. I also love seeing the visuals other people come up with, with or without digital aids. Fork this project and show us what Hopper Day means to you. Give it your own look, tell your story, or take it somewhere I haven't thought of.
