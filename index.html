The arc

Machine code → compilers → COBOL → talking to your computer. Every step Hopper pushed was about lowering the barrier between intent and software. Omarchy 4 plus natural-language agents is the next step in that same line. The five-year-old laptop is the proof, not the point.

The indictment

Around four hundred million PCs can't officially run Windows 11 — no TPM 2.0, no Secure Boot, unsupported CPU. Windows 10 support ended October 14, 2025. PIRG estimates up to one point six billion pounds of additional e-waste from machines that still work fine.

The same industry that spent decades preaching green goes quiet the moment its own power bill shows up. Carbon-tax talk vanished the second AI data centers needed electricity. Virginia started taxing data-center power in July 2026. Federal proposals followed in August. The hypocrisy is the point.

The opportunity

Grace Hopper's dream is our opportunity to make this better. Perfectly good hardware, a two-dollar stick, two minutes, and a machine that talks back — instead of a dump. This is ours to run with.
