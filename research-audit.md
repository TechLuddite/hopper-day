## History, installation, and launch claims

| Original claim | Finding and editorial decision |
|---|---|
| Omarchy 4 released August 14, 2026 | Supported by the release record. Retain the anniversary as the project's chosen date. |
| One of the most successful Linux launches ever | No cross-distribution metric or ranking established. Removed. |
| 100,000 downloads in the first week | The project reports 100,000 downloads “in the past week” in an August 28 post, two weeks after the release. That is not evidence for the first seven days after release. [Project announcement](https://omarchy.org/news/). Omitted from the landing page. |
| 200,000 downloads by day 18 | The September 2 announcement reports 200,000 ISO downloads in 18 days. Its elapsed-days wording does not align exactly with the August 14 release date. Attribute it as the project's report if used; do not treat downloads as unique users. [Announcement](https://omarchy.org/news/2026/09/quattro-crosses-200000-iso-downloads/). |
| ~$15M foundation pledges | The current launch announcement's title says $14.95M, while its URL says “8-million” and later news items describe progression through $10M, $12M, and $13M, plus token commitments. Do not reconstruct an original cash-pledge total from a mutable headline. Omit until amounts, dates, and cash versus in-kind commitments are reconciled. [Launch announcement](https://omarchy.org/news/2026/08/omacom-foundation-launches-with-8-million/); [news chronology](https://omarchy.org/news/). |
| DHH called it one of his greatest releases | Exact dated quotation not recovered. Omitted. |
| 38-second and 45-second installations | Individual timing claims were not independently established. The current manual provides a broader hardware-dependent estimate. Remove the stopwatch claims and simulated success output. |
| Any old PC is two minutes and a $2 USB stick away | Overstates compatibility, setup time, and cost. Remove. Link the installation guide instead. |
| No TPM 2.0 required / $0 TPM chip | The manual instructs disabling TPM/Secure Boot for installation. Replace the chip-price framing with a link to compatibility and setup guidance. Avoid implying that Windows incompatibility guarantees Linux compatibility. |
| Hopper's dream is finished; the barrier is gone | Editorial absolutes unsupported by a product release. Replace with a personal connection to Hopper's work and an invitation to make things. |
| August 14 was the day computers first spoke human | A celebratory slogan, not a historical claim. Replace the headline with an invitation to talk to your computer and explain the chosen anniversary. |

| Original claim | Finding and editorial decision |
|---|---|

## Research limits and follow-up

The retained landing-page figures have opened source material or downloaded official tables behind them. Search snippets alone were treated as leads for unresolved claims. For NOAA, the web reader failed but the official PDF downloaded successfully; its H258 table was checked in text and as an image. The APHIS official HTML table was downloaded and read. Large source documents were not copied into this repository; links, table locations, and the relevant amounts are recorded in research.md.

Optional future additions include a reproducible NIH type 1 research total, a G-FINDER data export, sector-specific PC replacement evidence, and a documented install or reuse story from a contributor. These should extend the argument rather than delay removal of unsupported claims. PRs with sources are welcome, as are forks that tell a different personal story about Hopper Day.
